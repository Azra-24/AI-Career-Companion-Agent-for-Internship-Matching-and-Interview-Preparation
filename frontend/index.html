✦ About CareerCompanion

Bridging the Gap Between Talent & Opportunities

CareerCompanion is an intelligent, end-to-end career assistance platform engineered to empower students and early-career engineers. By leveraging dense vector embeddings, grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and high-throughput LLMs, we transform the traditional, stressful job hunting process into a structured, data-driven journey.

✓
Semantic Vector Matching: Moving past rigid keyword filters with FAISS vector similarity search.
✓
Grounded RAG Intelligence: Role recommendations backed by verifiable fit rationale and skill-gap breakdowns.
✓
Comprehensive Preparation: Integrated ATS resume auditing, personalized cover letters, and multi-session AI mock interviews.
